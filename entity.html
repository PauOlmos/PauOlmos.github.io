ENTITY

The game



ENTITY is a videogame developed in Unity which explains the tragic story of an abused child via a boss rush. All its fears and traumatic events fight against the player adopting the form of a dark and sinister entity which will haunt the player until the end.



The project



This game was born to complete my degree in Design and Development of Videogames. As a final thesis, I focused in creating this work to give more storytelling to the boss rush games while analizing the best and worst bosses of the industry and developing a unique boss across multiple levels. With more than 300 pages of memory and 3h of gameplay, this project was honored with the highest score in the college (9.8/10).
