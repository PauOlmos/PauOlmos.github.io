THE BET

The game



"Guardians of the Galaxy: The Bet" is a title developed in college by the whole class with our own made videogame engine. This hack n' slash spacial adventure, sets Star Lord in a scenario where he needs to get an object from the wrong guys, leading to an epic adventure fighting Thanos and his army. The game contains multiple weapons, enemies, bosses and levels.



The project



This title was made in a few months from scratch by a 30 students team. Despite the start was very hard, as we focused primarly in developing the engine, we managed to have a great final game. My role in the team was to design and develop both of the game's bosses, being these, the best playable parts of the videogame.
