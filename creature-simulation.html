CREATURE SIMULATIONS

The project



This project is a part of a videogame which is still in development in which an explorer enters a region full of undiscovered creatures. I have developed a strcuture of scripts that create a simulation were all the cfreatures have their own life and interact with the world. The creatures can eat, sleep, walk around, play and fight against each other. All the stats and personalities of each creture are procedural, leading to a different outcome of each interaction everytime the player is nearby. The creatures tend to threat before attack, can run away if they are losing the fight, or do not search for combats if their species is not agressive at all. This structure is very versatil and unique, giving the opportunity to create a great amount of different creatures that make the world feel alive in a very short time. It is impossible to summarize all the possible outcomes, interactions and variables in a short paragraph, but as the game development progresses, the video will be more self-explanatory.
