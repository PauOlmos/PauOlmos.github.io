BLOODY WAR

The game



Bloody War is a turn-based RPG in which the player controlls a ww2 soldier lost in the depths of a haunted castle. My role in this game was the Producer and had to manage the whole development of the game from scratch in a team of 8 students. Despite all the production problems related to non efficient team memebers, I improved a lot my programming skills and learnt how to plan and produce a videogame and how to manage teams and their communication.
